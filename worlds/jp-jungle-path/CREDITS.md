# Jungle Path reference

Art direction and palette: the user-supplied image
`625885966_18110592436631809_5949542321660832326_n.webp` (1440 × 1241).
Original artist and license were not supplied. The source artwork is not redistributed.

Colours were sampled by median-cut quantization of five rectangular image regions:

- Upper-left foliage (70, 0)–(500, 190): olive `#6a7911`, green `#2b5a28`, `#1f702e`.
- Right foreground (900, 560)–(1340, 1120): emerald `#0b8442`, `#196b38`.
- Left middle (250, 260)–(600, 700): teal `#175e34`, `#0d4130`.
- Centre distance (620, 410)–(950, 810): blue `#0e273d`.
- Deep negative space: near-black green `#0e1818`.

All geometry, foliage, leaf mottling and dirt shading are original procedural code.
The seven-blade sprays interpret the reference's narrow, drooping leaf silhouettes;
no image, cutout, model, or texture from the painting is included in the room.
