# Asset Credits — Sector 9 Rain Corner (`doug-cassette-station`)

All 3D `.glb` assets in `assets/` are original uncompressed glTF 2.0 models generated procedurally in **Blender 5.3.0** via `tools/build_assets.py` by Doug (`douglascalhoun`):

- `assets/white_dragon_noodle_bar.glb` — Blade Runner–inspired open-air Neo-Tokyo noodle bar, stainless counter, corrugated awning, 6 padded swivel diner stools, exhaust ducts, copper stockpots, bowls & ribbed lanterns.
- `assets/neo_tokyo_tower.glb` — Layered brutalist megabuilding module with recessed windows, balconies, railings, exterior AC condensers, vertical pipes, water tank & rooftop beacon.
- `assets/electro_trolley.glb` — Sculpted beveled Electro-Punk streetcar with rail bogies, flanged wheels, HVAC pods, diamond scissor pantograph, and named bi-parting doors.
- `assets/cyber_scooter.glb` — Curved Akira/Neo-Tokyo sport cyber-scooter with telescopic forks, radial tires, glowing hubless rims, windscreen, handlebars, mirrors & courier case.
- `assets/cyber_human.glb` — Articulated cyberpunk trenchcoat human base with named `Head`, `Torso`, `ArmL`, `ArmR`, `LegL`, `LegR` pivots.
