    Controls & How to Play

    Portalverse uses first-person (FPS) mouse-look by default. Click the 3D view to lock your cursor, and press Esc anytime to free your cursor for buttons or menus.

    Movement & Flight

    • WASD / ArrowsWalk or steer in flight
    • MouseLook around (Pointer Lock)
    • SpaceJump (hold for higher jump)
    • Space ×2Jump again in mid-air to fly
    • Q (or Space)Rise up while flying
    • Hold EDescend & land on the ground
    • ShiftSprint on foot / Boost in flight
    • VSwitch 1st-person / 3rd-person
    • GToggle Classic / Platformer physics
    • EscUnlock cursor / close menus

    Portals & Co-op

    • Walk through or EEnter any glowing portal
    • R (or E near NPC)Talk to a room's Keeper
